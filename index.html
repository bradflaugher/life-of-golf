Terrain Guide

  • Sand stops a rolling ball. Only a Chip or Lob gets out.
  • Ice is frictionless: the ball slides until something stops it.
  • Mud is sticky rough that drains extra roll.
  • Ramps climb one tier uphill and add roll downhill.
  • Hedges bounce a rolling ball back. Bank shots welcome.
  • Boardwalks carry the ball one tile along the chevrons.
  • Toadstools spring the ball two tiles over anything.
  • Stone rings carry the ball to their twin and keep its momentum.
